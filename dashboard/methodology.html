TECHNICAL METHODOLOGY

How the Model Works

A hybrid ensemble architecture combining gradient-boosted classification, survival analysis, and explainable AI — continuously evaluated transparently against real performance benchmarks.

Risk Classification & Dataset Scope

Trained and benchmarked on 13,532 authentic baseline infrastructure corridors (live continuous training corpus: 13,586 corridors) spanning all 36 States & Union Territories (453 Districts) from MoRTH/NHAI, Indian Railways, and MNRE. A stacked ensemble of four gradient-boosted and tree-based models — XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost, and ExtraTrees — each trained independently, with their outputs combined by a logistic regression meta-learner. Predictions are calibrated using Platt scaling to ensure probability outputs are reliable, not just well-ranked.

Explainable AI

Every prediction is accompanied by a transparent explanation, not a black-box score. Meta-learner-weighted TreeSHAP attributions decompose each risk prediction into individual feature contributions, showing exactly which factors — terrain type, compensation disputes, clearance delays — pushed a project's risk score up or down, and by how much.

Statutory Regulatory Engine

RFCTLARR Act 2013 Statutory Rules & Milestone Engine

Land acquisition for public infrastructure in India is strictly governed by the Right to Fair Compensation and Transparency in Land Acquisition, Rehabilitation and Resettlement Act, 2013. Our engine explicitly models and tracks statutory compliance across the five legally mandated acquisition milestones:

SECTION 4 & 7 Mandatory

Social Impact Assessment (SIA)

Independent SIA study completed within 6 months, mandatory public hearings in affected areas, and evaluation by an independent Expert Group within 2 months of report submission.

Delay Impact: +45 to +60 days if appraisal stalls.
SECTION 11 & 19(7) Statutory Cliff-Edge

12-Month (365-Day) Lapse Clock

Section 11 Preliminary Notification triggers a strict 12-month legal window. If the Section 19 declaration of acquisition is not published within 365 days, proceedings lapse automatically by law.

Lapse Rule: Full acquisition process must restart from zero.
SECTION 26–30 Compensation

Market Multiplier & 100% Solatium

Market value determination multiplied by First Schedule factor (1.0x urban up to 2.0x rural). Section 30 mandates an obligatory 100% Solatium added to the total compensation package.

Statutory Guarantee: Solatium = 100% of market value.
SECTION 15 & 64 LARRA Adjudication

Title Disputes & Tribunal Appeals

Hearing of objections under Section 15 within 60 days. Any unaddressed title disputes or compensation claims are referred to the statutory Land Acquisition, Rehabilitation and Resettlement Authority (LARRA).

Empirical Delay: +2.5 days per 1% title dispute rate.

Survival Model Coupling (Uno C-Index 0.9060 ± 0.0020)

Statutory time constraints are decoupled from base feature transforms to eliminate data leakage, and instead synthesized via the post-inference Random Survival Forest timeline engine. This enables accurate, legal-grade bottleneck discovery and prescriptive intervention timing before statutory lapse thresholds occur.

Autonomous Continuous Learning & Model Governance

To prevent data drift and model staleness over dynamic infrastructure lifecycles, the National Risk Platform runs an autonomous background continuous learning daemon. Newly finalized corridor analyses and ground-truth completions are queued into the training store. The system automatically assesses Population Stability Index (PSI) drift, re-fits candidate ensembles, and enforces strict production validation gates before checkpoint promotion.
